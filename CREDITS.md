# CREDITS & ATTRIBUTIONS

## ESPORTS DYNASTY (Working Title)
A Portrait Mobile Management Sim built with Flutter and Flame.
Offline, original fictional IP set in the competitive esports scene of the Philippines.

### Visual Design & Art Direction
- **Character Chibi Sprites & Base Architecture**
  - Custom pixel art sprites for chibi esports athletes, coach staff, furniture, and isometric game room.
  - Procedural dynamic recolor tinting (skin tones, jersey colors, hair shading).
  - Procedural 2-color pixel shield emblem generator.

### Audio & Sound Effects
- **Chiptune Audio & SFX Design**
  - Named Events: `tap`, `confirm`, `cancel`, `coin`, `promotion`, `win`, `lose`, `popup`, `hit`, `week_tick`.
  - Background Music: `bgm_title`, `bgm_house`, `bgm_match`, `bgm_finals`.
  - License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal) Public Domain Dedication.
